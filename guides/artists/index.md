# For Artists

Everything you need to perform on Slipmat, one topic at a time:

- **[Artist Profile](/guides/artists/profile/)** — set up your profile, add members and roles, and
  manage your fans.
- **[Streaming & Going Live](/guides/artists/streaming/)** — get on the air, test your setup with a
  soundcheck, and keep an eye on your stream.
- **[Events](/guides/events/)** — the different kinds of events you can run and how each one works.
- **[How Events Start And End](/guides/events/lifecycle/)** — starting, ending, resuming, and
  everything Slipmat does automatically.
- **[Activity Log](/guides/activity-log/)** — the record of what happened to your artist and who did
  it.
- **[Chat](/guides/chat/)** — your artist channel, event chats, and moderation tools.
- **[AI Tools](/guides/ai-tools/)** — let an AI assistant like Claude manage your profile, events,
  and stats by asking in plain language.
