# How To

Short, practical walkthroughs. The first four are your first live event, in the order you'll need
them:

1. [Schedule Your First Event](/howto/artist/schedule-event/) — get it on the calendar.
2. [Set Up Song Requests](/howto/artist/set-up-requests/) — import your music and open requests.
3. [Test Your Stream](/howto/artist/test-streaming/) — a private soundcheck before an audience shows
   up.
4. [Run Your Event](/howto/artist/run-your-show/) — the night itself, from the Stream Dashboard.
