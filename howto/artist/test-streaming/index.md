# How To Test Your Stream

A **soundcheck** is a private test run of your stream. It's unlisted and reachable only by its own
link, it never shows up in Slipmat's listings, it doesn't mark you as live, and it isn't part of your
event history. Use it to hear and see exactly what your audience would, before there is an audience.

## Run a soundcheck

1. Open **Soundcheck** in your artist menu, under Plan — <https://slipmat.io/artist/stream/> — and
   start one **before** you connect your streaming software. A soundcheck can only start when nothing
   else of yours is live.
2. Connect your encoder using the Streaming Server URL and Stream Key shown on the same page. Your
   video appears on the soundcheck page once the stream connects.
3. Open the soundcheck page and listen. Share the link if you'd like a second pair of ears.
4. Watch it in the [Stream Dashboard](/howto/artist/run-your-show/), which runs a soundcheck like any
   other event. The ring shows whether Slipmat is receiving your stream, and anything it finds wrong
   — low audio bitrate, mono audio, no audio, an unsuitable resolution, dropped frames — is listed
   underneath. Fix those in your encoder now: your listeners get exactly the stream the dashboard is
   checking.
5. **End soundcheck** when you're done. If you forget, Slipmat ends it 15 minutes after the last
   stream disconnects.

::: tip Start the soundcheck first
Start the soundcheck, then connect your stream. The other way round and you may go live for real —
with no scheduled event of yours starting soon, a connecting stream begins an ad hoc event.
:::
